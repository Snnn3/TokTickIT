# TokTickIT Service Desk Context

This glossary records the domain language confirmed while studying the Lab 4 service-desk workflow.

## Ticket workflow

**Ticket Owner**:
The single primary staff member responsible for coordinating a Ticket as a whole.
_Avoid_: Treating every staff member who works on the Ticket as its owner.

**Action Taken**:
A work-record entry belonging to exactly one Ticket that documents work performed, its result, and any follow-up information.
_Avoid_: Treating an Action Taken as a second Ticket.

**Action Performer**:
The authenticated IT Staff member who records an Action Taken. The Action Performer may be different from the Ticket Owner.
_Avoid_: Assuming the Ticket Owner must perform every Action Taken.

**Requester resolution indication**:
A Requester’s statement that the problem appears resolved; it is advisory and does not change the Ticket status. IT Staff must review the work and make the formal status transition.
_Avoid_: Treating Requester confirmation as permission to resolve or close a Ticket.
